# Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism

[日本語](../../jp-mlit/README.md)

Road and transport data of the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT). National Land Numerical Information and PLATEAU have many datasets with their own terms, so they are separate stewards ([jp-ksj](../jp-ksj/README.md) and jp-plateau). The terms of use are the [terms of use of the MLIT website](https://www.mlit.go.jp/link.html), based on the Public Data License (Version 1.0) and compatible with CC BY 4.0. See the README of each collection.
