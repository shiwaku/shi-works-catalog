# National Land Numerical Information

[日本語](../../jp-ksj/README.md)

The National Land Numerical Information (KSJ), maintained and published by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism: basic geospatial data on Japan such as administrative boundaries and schools. Terms of use are set per dataset. Most are CC BY 4.0 or the KSJ [terms of use](https://nlftp.mlit.go.jp/ksj/other/agreement_01.html), which follow the Government of Japan Standard Terms of Use (Version 2.0). See the README of each collection.

| Collection | Contents |
|---|---|
| [Administrative boundaries](admin-boundaries/README.md) | Municipality boundaries (wards for designated cities). As of 1 January 2026, 1,905 rows |
